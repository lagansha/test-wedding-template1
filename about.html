About Lagansha — a family-run Bengali matchmaking desk in Serampore

Who we are, how our offline service actually works, what we verify, what we charge, and who to contact if something is wrong.

Who we are

Lagansha is an offline Bengali matrimonial service — a modern ghatak desk — based in Serampore, West Bengal. We work with families across Hooghly, Howrah and Kolkata who would rather speak to a person than scroll through thousands of public profiles.

We are intentionally small. There is one phone number, one email address and one team handling your biodata from the first call to the introduction. We do not run a profile marketplace, we do not sell data, and we do not claim branches we do not have.

Team: Lagansha is run by a small, family-run team based in Serampore, West Bengal.

What “offline” means here

Your biodata is not published on a searchable website. It is kept in our own records and shown only to a family you have agreed to consider — and only after we have spoken to both sides. Many families first contact us by phone or WhatsApp and share the minimum details needed to begin.

What we verify — and what we do not

We check the identity and biodata details a family shares with us, and we have a direct conversation before any introduction. Lagansha does not conduct police verification, forensic checks or background investigations. Before finalising a match, you and your guardians must independently verify the other party’s character, documents and claims. We would rather state this plainly than use the word “verified” loosely.

Consent, privacy and your rights

Registration is consent-based. You can withdraw consent and ask us to permanently delete your biodata at any time, at no cost. We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or passwords. Our Privacy Policy and FAQ explain data retention, cookies and third-party services in plain language.

Fees and transparency

Registration is free. If a match, roka or engagement is fixed through Lagansha, a success fee becomes payable; the amount is told to you in writing before you proceed. We are an introduction service: we do not guarantee marriage, and we are not responsible for disputes or financial loss between families after the marriage.
