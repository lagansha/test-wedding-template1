Our offline Bengali matchmaking services in Hooghly & Howrah

Biodata registration, personal shortlisting, community-wise search, second marriage and NRI introductions — all handled privately by one desk in Serampore.

1. Free biodata registration

Our registration form collects the details that actually matter for a match: age, height, education, occupation, family, lifestyle, optional horoscope details and partner preferences. It takes about ten minutes. The form is delivered to us by email through Web3Forms and is never published as a public profile.

2. Personal screening and shortlisting

We read every biodata ourselves and speak with the family before shortlisting. Matching considers age, education, occupation, family values, location and the preferences you recorded — not just a keyword filter. An introduction is proposed only when both families have agreed.

3. Community-wise (জাতি) Bengali matchmaking

Bengali families often have community preferences — Brahmin, Kayastha, Baidya, Mahishya, Sadgop, Subarnabanik, Gandhabanik, Tili, Tanti, Ugra Kshatriya, Gop / Goala and others. We note your preference at registration and shortlist accordingly, while keeping every introduction consent-based and respectful. All religions are welcome.

4. Second marriage (পুনর্বিবাহ) introductions

We handle divorced, widowed and separated profiles with the same privacy and dignity as first marriages. Marital status is declared accurately and shared only with the families you agree to meet.

5. NRI Bengali introductions

For Bengali families settled abroad, we coordinate across time zones by WhatsApp and email, and align shortlisting with a visit-to-India window when there is one. Horoscope and community preferences are recorded the same way as for local profiles.

6. Service for all religions

Lagansha works with families of all religions and castes. We ask only that every applicant is 18 or older and legally free to marry, and that the information shared is accurate.

Process

How an introduction actually happens

Five clear steps, with consent at every hand-off.

First conversation

A phone call or WhatsApp message to understand the family, expectations and timeline. No biodata needed yet.

Private biodata

Share details through the free form or over a call. We confirm consent and note what may be shared later.

Shortlisting by a person

We compare preferences, community, education, occupation and location, then speak to the other family.

Consent-based introduction

Only the details you approved are passed on. Both sides decide whether to meet; nobody is pressured.

Independent verification by you

Verify documents, character and claims yourself before finalising. We tell you exactly where our checking stops.

What we do not do

  • No public, searchable profile gallery or open biodata database.
  • No police verification, forensic checks or private investigators.
  • No sale or rental of your contact number or biodata.
  • No guarantee of marriage, and no liability for disputes after marriage.
  • No Aadhaar, PAN, bank or password collection on this website.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies — see our cookie answer.
Important: “Verified” is a word many matrimonial sites use loosely. We do not use it. Read our verification policy before you decide.
