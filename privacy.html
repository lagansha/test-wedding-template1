Privacy Policy & consent

Plain-language explanation of what we collect, why we keep it, who receives it and how to have it deleted. Effective 2026-11-24.

1. Who we are

Lagansha (Lagansha Offline Matrimonial Services) is an offline Bengali matrimonial service based in Serampore, West Bengal, India. For any privacy question, contact the Grievance & Privacy Desk at lagansha10@gmail.com or +91 80105 64304.

2. What we collect and why

We collect only matrimonial biodata: name, mobile number, age and date of birth, gender, height, marital status, religion, mother tongue, caste / sub-caste, education, occupation, income range, family details, appearance and lifestyle details, optional horoscope details, address / locality and partner preferences. This is used solely to shortlist a suitable match and to speak with both families.

We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, payment card information or passwords on this website. Applicants must be 18 or older; we do not knowingly collect data about minors.

3. Consent

We process your biodata on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you submit the registration form or share details with us. You may withdraw that consent at any time by phone or email. Withdrawal and deletion are free of charge and we will stop all introductions.

4. How long data is kept and who receives it

Your biodata is retained until the match is finalised or until you ask us to delete it, whichever is earlier. It is held in our own offline records with restricted access. We never sell your data. Your contact number and biodata are shared with another family only after your permission, and only to the extent you agreed to.

Third-party processor: the registration form is delivered to us by email through Web3Forms. The details you type in the form are transmitted to Web3Forms and then emailed to us; its handling of that transmission is governed by Web3Forms’ own privacy policy. Please do not share information you would not be comfortable sending by email.

5. Cookies, embeds and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising, login or session cookies, does not run tracking scripts, and does not store your contact details in browser storage.

The Contact page contains a Google Maps embed. It is not loaded until you click “Show Google Map”. Once loaded, Google may set its own cookies and collect usage data under Google’s privacy policy. If you do not want that, simply do not click the button — the phone number, email address and map link remain usable without it.

6. Security and breach notification

Access to records is restricted to the Lagansha team. No online system can be guaranteed perfectly secure; if a data leak or security breach occurs, we will promptly inform affected families and the appropriate authority, and tell you what information was involved.

7. Verification limits and liability

We check the identity and biodata details a family shares with us and speak with both sides before an introduction. Lagansha does not conduct police verification, forensic checks or background investigations. Independently verifying the other party’s character, documents and claims is the responsibility of the client and their guardians.

We are an introduction service. We do not guarantee marriage and we are not responsible for disputes, financial loss or personal issues between the two families after the marriage. Registration is free; a success fee, disclosed in writing in advance, applies if a match, roka or engagement is fixed through us.

8. Your rights

  • Ask what information we hold about you.
  • Ask for incorrect details to be corrected.
  • Withdraw consent and ask for permanent deletion, at no cost.
  • Ask us to stop all introductions.
